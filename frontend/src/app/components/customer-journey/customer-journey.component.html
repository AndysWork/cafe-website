Turn attention into action

High interest, low conversion: at least 5 viewing sessions and fewer than 10% ordering that item. This is a review signal, not proof of a pricing problem.

Detail views: opening the dish or customization panel. Landing-page item clicks are clicks, not fabricated detail views.

Viewer rates: unique viewing sessions that later add or order the item, divided by viewing sessions. Quantities include attributed orders even if the customer skipped the detail view.

Cancelled / removed orders are excluded from net quantities. These are consented website orders, not all web or Zomato sales; use Business Analytics for complete sales.

How to read these numbers

A cart is abandoned only when items remain, there is no recorded order, and the session has been inactive for at least 30 minutes. Removed-all-items carts are not abandoned.

Sessions that continue beyond the selected end date are not declared abandoned. Cancelled orders remain order placements but are removed from net order value and item quantities.

The sequential funnel requires menu → cart → checkout → order in that order. Restored carts and direct checkout may skip stages, so its final count can be lower than overall conversions.

Coverage & privacy

Only consented, successfully received events are measured. These counts are not all website visitors or all orders. Ad blockers, connection failures and browser shutdowns may create gaps.

Detailed data are retained for at most 30 days and excluded from queries after expiry, even before MongoDB's TTL cleanup runs. Account identity is stored only after separate permission.

Locations, referrers, IP-derived geography and unnecessary personal data are not collected. Staff/admin sessions are separated. Reports and CSV exports are admin-only and outlet-scoped.

Very large queries fail explicitly at 20,000 sessions or 200,000 events. Narrow the date range; figures are never silently truncated.
